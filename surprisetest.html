Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes, you can try us for free for 30 days. If you want, we'll provide you with a free, personalized 30-minute onboarding call to get you up and running as soon as possible.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes, you can easily upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Once canceled, you'll still have access until the end of your billing cycle.

Can other info be added to an invoice?

Yes, you can add custom details like your company VAT or billing address directly to invoices.

How does billing work?

Plans are billed on a monthly or annual basis depending on your preference. You'll receive an invoice via email.

How do I change my account email?

You can update your email address anytime from the profile section within your account settings.

Still have questions?

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Please chat to our friendly team.
